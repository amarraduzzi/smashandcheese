About Smash'N Cheese

At Smash'N Cheese, everything starts with the same move: a ball of fresh beef, smashed onto a scorching-hot griddle. The intense heat instantly caramelizes the surface while the inside stays juicy — that's what gives a smash burger its signature taste.

We don't stop at the beef: our buns, fries and desserts (chocolate mousse, tiramisu) are all made in-house, every day, in our Agdal kitchen.

Whether it's a quick lunch dine-in, takeout, a phone order, WhatsApp, or Glovo delivery, we hold the same standard: a burger smashed to order, ready as fast as possible.
